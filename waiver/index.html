The program includes choir performances (nastupi horova), folklore group performances (nastupi folklornih grupa), sports (basketball, volleyball, soccer), lectures, and the daily prayers in congregation. The gathering runs over several days with overnight stays as arranged by the organizers.

2 Islamic manners & prayers

Please read these with your child. Every participant agrees to:

  • Behave with Islamic manners (adab): be honest, kind and respectful to the imams, organizers, volunteers, hosts and every other participant.
  • Pray the five daily prayers in congregation when they are called, and attend the lectures and program sessions on time.
  • Dress modestly at all times, including during sports.
  • Keep the proper Islamic boundaries between boys and girls, and stay in the rooms and areas assigned to them.
  • Never leave the venue or the group without permission from a group leader.
  • No smoking, vaping, alcohol, drugs, weapons, fighting, bullying or inappropriate language.
  • Use phones respectfully and put them away during prayers, lectures and performances.
  • Look after the venue and other people's belongings. Damage caused on purpose will be charged to the family.
