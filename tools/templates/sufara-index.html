🔤 Sufara – the Arabic letters

Learn each letter: how it looks, how it sounds, and Qur’an words that use it – with a short video and a game. Green tiles are reading rules (sukun, tanwin, long vowels…). After every 7 letters there is a review. Watch all videos in the Sufara playlist.

Letter sounds explained for English-speaking children. Qur’an words and recitation: Quran.com. Videos: “Sufara” by Amsal Memic, with hfz. Nermin Spahić.
